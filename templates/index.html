ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About Shield Pay

A cybersecurity-focused prototype designed to make payment QR checks understandable and accessible.

Shield Pay is a cybersecurity-focused prototype designed to help users identify potentially tampered payment QR codes before completing a transaction.

Our Goal

Help users become more aware of QR-code-based payment threats and take a moment to review what they are paying.

Security First

Provide a straightforward verification workflow before a user leaves the app to make a payment elsewhere.

Designed for Safer Digital Payments

Make QR verification understandable and accessible with readable statuses and actionable context.
